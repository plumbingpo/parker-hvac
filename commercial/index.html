Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Parker HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your operating hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. You get detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Parker HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Parker HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Parker and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
