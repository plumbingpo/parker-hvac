Keeping Your Denver HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of summer.

Small problems don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.
